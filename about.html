Lumen Press

A personal shelf for books.

A small browser-based catalog for finding, saving, and keeping track of books.

Book records

Catalog search and book metadata come from Open Library. Cover images are served by Open Library Covers. Information can be incomplete or vary between records.

Your library

Authentication uses Firebase. When you sign in, your saved books, favorites, reading status, and reading progress sync to your private Firestore library. Guest data stays in this browser until you sign in.

QR codes

Book codes point to the corresponding details page. The scanner accepts recognized catalog identifiers and same-site details links; it does not forward arbitrary scanned URLs.
